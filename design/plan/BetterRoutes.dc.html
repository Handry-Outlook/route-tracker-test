New rider controls
Avoid hillsQuiet roadsTailwind on the way home Paved onlyAllow gravelBack before sunset
Avoid hillsPenalises candidates by metres climbed per km, using the elevation data already fetched. Quiet roadsDown-weights primary/secondary OSM classes, up-weights cycleway, living_street, track. Tailwind homeUses the live wind bearing the app already reads, and orients the loop so the return leg runs with the wind. Back before sunsetCaps the distance band from remaining daylight and your average speed.
Why this route

Each candidate carries a one-line reason drawn from its real scores, so the choice is explainable rather than three near-identical options: "Most cycleway (68%), 90 m less climbing, but 4 km longer."
