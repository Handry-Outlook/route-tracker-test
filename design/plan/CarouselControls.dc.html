Horizontal decks on a laptop
Today .hscroll sets scrollbar-width: none and hides the WebKit scrollbar. With a mouse there is no affordance and often no way to scroll at all — the carousel is unusable on a laptop.

Rules
ArrowsShown only on pointer devices (@media (hover:hover)). Scroll by one card width. Disabled at each end. ScrollbarThin, always visible on desktop — the honest affordance. Hidden on touch where swipe is native. WheelVertical wheel maps to horizontal scroll while the pointer is over a deck. DragClick-and-drag to pan, with a grab cursor. KeyboardDeck is focusable; Left/Right arrows move one card, Home/End jump to the ends. Counter"2 / 6" beside the section title so position is always legible.
Better still on wide screens

Above 1100px the recommended loops stop being a carousel at all and become a two-column grid — no scrolling needed, every option visible at once. The carousel treatment stays for the narrower community rails.
