# Ridewise Feature Inventory
**Build:** v4.90

This is the canonical feature baseline. Future releases must preserve every applicable item.

- PWA installation, service worker, Wake Lock, responsive mobile/desktop UI.
- Point-to-point Start, Finish, current address, multiple removable waypoints, alternatives and map selection.
- Adventure distance range, optional waypoints, 120 m sampled overlap detection, strict retrace rejection, compactness scoring, and candidate deduplication.
- Route card Save, Share and Animation Preview, saved-route management, GPX import and GPX export.
- Route editing, undo history, 3D preview and Google Maps preview.
- Navigation voice toggle, iPhone speech unlock, turn guidance, rerouting, four-second heading smoothing, wrong-way delay, map bearing and route alternatives.
- Progress-aware route colours for travelled, next and future sections.
- Pause/Resume Recording and Navigation with staged End controls.
- Live journey creation, pause/resume, follower status and shared rider position.
- Activity naming, six photos, editing, sharing, social PNG, route reuse, sorting and detailed speed/elevation/wind/power profiles.
- Rider profile, estimated power, W/kg and effort score.
- Spatial wind, radar, and real current conditions with compass direction, mean wind and gust; optional Xweather with Open-Meteo fallback.
- Email/password and Google authentication.
- Right-click/long-press map location actions and map Share control.

- Three-day hourly weather forecast.
- CyclOSM infrastructure overlay and Overpass-derived route infrastructure scoring.
- Real route wind components with green tailwind and red headwind bars.
- Direct live-progress sharing from the map Share dialog.

- Ridewise branding and tagline: Plan smarter. Ride better.
- Direction arrows along route geometry.
- Motorway rejection and unnecessary crossing/cycle-detour penalties.

- Fast-first Adventure: one initial request, user-triggered alternatives.
- Mid-navigation optional recommendations remain selectable with time/distance explanations.
